## Regulatory Compliance

### For Node Operators

1. **KYC/AML**: Node operators may be subject to KYC/AML regulations depending on jurisdiction
2. **Money Transmitter**: Running a node may be considered money transmission in some jurisdictions
3. **Tax Reporting**: Cryptocurrency earnings may be taxable

### For Users

1. **Custodial Risk**: Use non-custodial wallets when possible
2. **Private Keys**: Never share private keys
3. **Smart Contracts**: Audit contracts before interacting

## For Exchanges

Exchanges listing NEO must:
1. Obtain proper licensing
2. Implement KYC/AML
3. Secure cold storage
4. Maintain insurance (recommended)
